# LCF Website — Simple Guide for Your Team

This guide explains how to view your public website and how to use the Admin Portal to update it. No technical knowledge is needed — just follow the steps.

---

## 1. Your Public Website

These are the pages visitors see. You don't need to do anything to "publish" the website itself — it updates automatically whenever you make changes in the Admin Portal (see Section 3).

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Home | Main landing page with an overview of LCF |
| About | Information about LCF's mission |
| Our People | Team members and external roles (advisors, patrons, etc.) |
| Events | Upcoming and past events |
| Get Involved | Ways to volunteer or partner with LCF |
| News | News articles and insights |
| Contact | Contact details / contact form |

---

## 2. Logging Into the Admin Portal

1. Go to: `your-website.com/admin/login`
2. Enter your **email** and **password**, then click **Sign In**.
3. A 6-digit security code will be sent to your email. Enter it within 10 minutes to complete login.

### Forgot your password?
1. On the login page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and submit. You'll receive a reset link by email.
3. Open the email and follow the link to set a new password.

---

## 3. The Admin Dashboard

After logging in, you'll land on the **Dashboard**. It gives you a quick snapshot of your site: how many team members, events, news articles, and quotes you have, and how many are published vs. hidden.

On the left is a **menu** with all the sections you can manage. On mobile, tap the menu icon (☰) at the top to open it.

---

## 4. Managing Content

For every section below, the same basic actions apply:
- **Add**: Click the orange "+ Add" button, fill in the form, and save.
- **Edit**: Click the pencil/edit icon next to an item.
- **Publish / Unpublish**: Use the eye icon to control whether something is visible on the public website. "Unpublished" items are hidden from visitors but not deleted.
- **Delete**: Click the trash icon. You'll be asked to confirm — this cannot be undone.

### Sections in the menu:

- **Team Members (Our People)** — Add or edit your internal team profiles (name, role, photo). Drag items to reorder how they appear on the public page.
- **Events** — Add upcoming events, edit details, or mark events as published/draft.
- **Testimonials** — Reviews submitted by visitors. Approve, reject, edit, or delete them before they appear on the site.
- **News & Insights** — Write and publish news articles.
- **Leaders' Quotes** — Manage inspirational quotes. You can also click **Invite Leader** to email someone a link so they can submit their own quote for approval.
- **Volunteer Applications** — View applications from people wanting to volunteer. Click the envelope icon to email an applicant directly. Click the arrow to see full details.
- **Partners & Supporters** — Manage logos/info of partner organisations.
- **External Roles** — Manage advisors, patrons, brand ambassadors, honorary supporters, and mentees shown on the public "Our People" page.
- **Settings** — View your account name/email, change your password, or log out.
- **Users** *(only visible to Super Admins)* — Add new admin accounts, change someone's role, or remove an admin.

---

## 5. Notifications

The bell icon at the top of the Admin Portal shows recent activity (e.g., new volunteer applications, new testimonials). Click it to view, mark as read, or clear notifications.

---

## 6. Logging Out

Always log out when you're done, especially on a shared or public computer:
1. Go to **Settings** in the left menu.
2. Click the red **Logout** button.

---

## 7. Good Practices

- Only share admin login details with trusted staff.
- Use a strong, unique password and change it periodically via **Settings > Change Password**.
- Double-check content before publishing — published items go live on the public website immediately.
- If unsure about deleting something, unpublish it first instead — this hides it without permanently removing it.

---

## 8. Need Help?

If something looks broken or you're unsure how to do something, contact your website developer/support contact before making major changes.
